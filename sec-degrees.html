    Section 2.4 Degrees

    Convention 2.4.1. Notation.

    We denote the number of degrees in an angle \(\angle BAC\) or \(\alpha\) by the symbol \(|\angle BAC|\text{,}\) or \(|\alpha|\text{,}\) as the case may be.
    🔗
    🔗

    Axiom 2.4.2. Protractor Axiom.

    The number of degrees in an angle (also known as its degree-measure) is always a number between \(0^\circ\) and \(360^\circ\text{.}\) The number of degrees of an ordinary angle is less than \(180^\circ\text{.}\) It has these properties:
    🔗
    1. A straight angle has \(180^\circ\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    2. Given a ray \([AB\text{,}\) and a number \(d\) between \(0\) and \(180\text{,}\) there is exactly one ray from \(A\) on each side of the line \(AB\) that makes an (ordinary) angle having \(d\) degrees with the ray \([AB\text{.}\)
      🔗
      🔗
    3. If \(D\) is a point inside an angle \(\angle BAC\text{,}\) then
      \begin{equation*} |\angle BAC| = |\angle BAD| + |\angle DAC|. \end{equation*}
      🔗
      🔗
    Null angles are assigned \(0^\circ\text{,}\) full angles \(360^\circ\text{,}\) and reflex angles have more than \(180^\circ\text{.}\) If \(A\text{,}\) \(B\text{,}\) and \(C\) are noncollinear points, then the reflex angle outside the angle \(\angle BAC\) measures \(360^\circ - |\angle BAC|\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    Definition 2.4.3.

    The ray \([AD\) is the bisector of the angle \(\angle BAC\) if
    \begin{equation*} |\angle BAD| = |\angle DAC| = \frac{|\angle BAC|}{2}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    Definition 2.4.4.

    A right angle is an angle of exactly \(90^\circ\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗

    Exercises Exercises

    1.

    How many degrees are in a straight angle?
    🔗
    Hint.
    See the Axiom 2.4.2.
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(180^\circ\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    Solution.
    By the Protractor Axiom, a straight angle has \(180^\circ\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    2.

    An angle \(\angle BAC\) has \(70^\circ\text{.}\) The ray \([AD\) bisects it. Find \(|\angle BAD|\text{.}\)
    🔗
    Hint.
    The bisector divides the angle into two equal halves.
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(35^\circ\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    Solution.
    By the definition of angle bisector, \(|\angle BAD| = \frac{|\angle BAC|}{2} = \frac{70^\circ}{2} = 35^\circ\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    3.

    An ordinary angle \(\angle BAC\) has \(110^\circ\text{.}\) What is the measure of the reflex angle outside it?
    🔗
    Hint.
    The ordinary angle and its reflex angle together make a full angle.
    🔗
    🔗
    Answer.
    \(250^\circ\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
    Solution.
    The reflex angle measures \(360^\circ - 110^\circ = 250^\circ\text{.}\)
